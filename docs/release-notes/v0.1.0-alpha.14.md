**A turn's outcome says how its last round ended** (#190). `TurnOutcome` gained
`round_stop`, the provider's own stop reason for the round whose text is the reply, so an
embedder with no event observer can tell a turn cut off at `max_tokens` from one that
finished — the two produce byte-identical messages, and until now only a caller rendering
the stream could tell them apart. It is reported and not acted on: the loop still decides
re-entry on the presence of tool calls, never on the reason, and the figure is orthogonal to
`TurnStop`, so a turn that ran out of rounds and was cut at `max_tokens` in its last round
reports both. `None` means no round completed. `agent-run` now reads the flag off the
outcome instead of latching it off the stream, which changes one line of its output: a
wrap-up round cut at `--max-tokens` no longer reports the cut, the run having already said
that round's text was neither an answer nor saved.

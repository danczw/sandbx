**Every audit record gained fields, and a reader pairing a spawn with its exit
by position has to use them instead.** `spawned`, `exited`, `failed` and
`degraded` each carry `spawn=`, a per-process nonce ahead of a counter, and
`session=`; `spawned` also carries `args=`, capped at 512 rendered characters
and last on the line (#249). A guard that should name its session on those
records is built with `FsGuard::for_session` or `ExecutionContext::for_session`,
there being no setter: the record is written while the guard is built, so one
told afterwards has already reported without a session. `FsGuard::read_dir`
returns an owned `Listing` rather than `std::fs::ReadDir`, and `new` keeps its
signature on both types (#230).

**On Linux 5.6 and above, a filesystem access sandbx makes on its own behalf is
resolved beneath a descriptor it holds on the granted root**, so a directory
swapped in after the policy check is refused rather than reached; below 5.6, and
for a grant naming a file, the older two-syscall window remains and
`SECURITY.md` says so (#230). `SECURITY.md` also now defines the three severity
rungs a security issue takes, a label on a weakness rather than a change to the
sandbox (#426), and the six refusals behind its default-root claim are published
rather than private (#413).

**The workspace is 26 crates where alpha.18 had seven, and four names are
gone:** `sandbx-agent`, `sandbx-session` and `sandbx-tui` dissolved into their
parts and `sandbx-providers` is now `sandbx-anthropic`, so a dependency or link
naming any of the four re-points — `context/guide-repo-map.md` is the index
(#332). Unlike phase 2, visibility did widen: 27 baselines moved 1,032 lines in
and 967 out while 55 items became newly reachable, 34 of them not derive or
auto-trait impls and each now crossing a crate line. `sandbx-core`'s own
baseline was untouched by all 22 extractions; its 70 added lines are the two
changes above. CI now fails when `deny.toml`'s allowlist disagrees with the real
graph, the mistyped block name that ungated a crate at exit 0 included (#416).
